The Corner Strategy

The most important rule in 2048: Pick a corner and keep your highest tile there at all times.

Most players choose the bottom-left or bottom-right corner. Once you choose, never let your highest tile leave that position.

How to Implement:

  1. Choose a corner (e.g., bottom-left)
  2. Use only two primary directions (e.g., down and left)
  3. Use the third direction (right) only when absolutely necessary
  4. Never use the opposite direction (up) unless forced

The Snake Pattern

Build your tiles in a snake-like pattern from the corner. This keeps tiles organized and maximizes merge opportunities.

For a bottom-left corner strategy:

  • Highest tile in bottom-left
  • Second highest to its right
  • Continue along the bottom row
  • Then snake up to the next row

Essential Tips

  • Think ahead: Plan 2-3 moves in advance
  • Avoid trapping tiles: Don't let small tiles get stuck between large ones
  • Keep the board open: Maintain empty spaces for maneuvering
  • Don't chase merges: Sometimes it's better to wait for a better opportunity
  • Stay calm: When the board fills up, don't panic - look for the best move

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Moving your highest tile out of the corner
  • Using all four directions randomly
  • Not planning ahead
  • Getting tunnel vision on one merge
  • Panicking when the board gets full

Practice Makes Perfect

Start with 5×5 mode to practice strategies with more room for error. Once comfortable, try the standard 4×4, then challenge yourself with 3×3 hard mode!
