About 5×5 Mode

The 5×5 mode offers a more relaxed experience with 25 cells instead of 16. The extra space gives you more room to maneuver and plan your moves, making it perfect for beginners or when you want to achieve very high scores.

Want to learn strategies? Check out our complete guide with strategies and tips.

5×5 Strategy Tips

  • Take advantage of the extra space to build higher tile values.
  • Perfect for practicing the snake pattern strategy.
  • Aim for 4096 or even 8192 — it's much more achievable in this mode!
  • Experiment with different strategies since you have more room to recover from mistakes.
