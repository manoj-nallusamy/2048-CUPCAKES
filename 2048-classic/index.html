How to Play 2048

Use your arrow keys (or swipe on mobile) to move the tiles. When two tiles with the same number collide, they merge into one with the combined value. Reach the 2048 tile to win, then keep going for a higher score!

Want to master the game? Check out our complete guide with strategies and tips.

Strategy Tips

  • Pick a corner and keep your highest-value tile anchored there.
  • Avoid moves that break your stack; prefer two-direction play (e.g., only left and down).
  • Look for safe merges that don't trap low-value tiles.
  • Think one move ahead to prevent filling the board.
